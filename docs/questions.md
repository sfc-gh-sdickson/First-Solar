# First Solar Agent - Test Questions

30+ complex questions organized by domain for testing the First Solar Supply Chain Intelligence Agent.

## Inventory & Coverage

1. What is the total inventory value by plant for the latest snapshot?
2. Which materials are below safety stock levels at any plant?
3. What is the average days forward coverage by material category?
4. Which critical materials have less than 30 days of forward coverage?
5. Show me the top 10 materials by inventory value at the Ohio 1 plant.
6. Which materials have days forward coverage below their lead time?

## Procurement & Expediting

7. What is the total value of open purchase orders by plant?
8. What are the total expediting costs for rush orders?
9. Which suppliers drive the most expediting cost?
10. How many purchase orders are overdue (expected delivery passed but still In Transit)?
11. What is the average delivery variance by supplier for received POs?
12. Show me all rush orders placed in the last 30 days and their costs.

## Supplier Risk & Performance

13. Which suppliers have the worst on-time delivery performance?
14. Show me high-risk suppliers (risk score > 4) supplying critical materials.
15. Which suppliers are Tier2 and what materials do they supply?
16. What is the quality rejection rate by supplier?
17. Has 5N Plus had any supply disruptions or quality issues recently?
18. What logistics disruptions have affected our CdTe supply chain?

## Supply Recommendations

19. Which materials have critical or high priority supply recommendations?
20. What is the total estimated cost to resolve all critical recommendations?
21. Show me transfer recommendations where freight cost is lower than a new PO.
22. How many recommendations are unactioned?
23. What triggered the critical recommendations for semiconductor materials?

## Manufacturing Schedule

24. What is the manufacturing schedule adherence by plant?
25. Which weeks had production quantity reduced and why?
26. Show me all pull-forward schedule changes and their reasons.
27. What is the total volume impact of schedule changes by change type?
28. Why was production reduced at the Ohio plant in January?

## Demand Forecasting

29. What is the demand forecast for Series 6 modules at Arizona for the next 8 weeks?
30. What is the demand forecast for Series 7 across all plants?
31. Which materials are at highest risk of stockout based on current inventory?

## Cross-Domain & Executive

32. Give me an executive summary of supply chain health (KPI dashboard).
33. Which materials are single-source with no substitutes? What is their current coverage?
34. How does our semiconductor material lead time compare to industry benchmarks?
35. If we lost our CdTe supplier for 30 days, what would the production impact be?
36. Connect the supplier delay anomalies to affected purchase orders — what is the total value at risk?
